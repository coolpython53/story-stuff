My Crush's Dog Is How We Met...

Every day, my crush's dog ran over to my house.

At first I didn't even know it was her dog. He'd just show up in my yard, sit by my door, and refuse to leave.

I'd give him water, play with him for a bit, and then he'd run back home.

Then one day, she came running down the street looking for him, all panicked — and there I was, holding her dog.

She goes, "He never runs to anyone else's house."

Now he still runs over every day.

Except now... she comes with him.
