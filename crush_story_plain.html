I thought my crush secretly hated me.

Every time I tried to talk to her in the hallway, she'd go quiet and walk away with her friends.

I texted her one time and she left me on read for a whole day. A WHOLE day. I was staring at my phone like an idiot.

Then at lunch I sat at her table and she literally got up and moved.

So I'm thinking, okay, she hates me, I'm never trying again, I'm actually embarrassed.

Then her best friend tells me, "She thinks you don't like her."

Apparently she kept walking away because her friends would not stop teasing her about me, and she got shy. And she left me on read because she kept typing and deleting her reply.

She didn't hate me at all. She liked me back, she was just as nervous as I was.
